Namaa — Tadawul Investment Tools

Four decision tools for Tadawul-listed companies, running entirely in this page against the real IS499 database dump — no server, no install, nothing sent anywhere.

Not investment advice. Every number here comes from historical filings and a Random Forest model trained on 185 companies (see the Growth tool for the model card). Treat outputs as a ranking signal, not a forecast — check filings before acting on anything.

Recommend Dividend Companies

Give it an amount and a split between coverage (how many months of the year the portfolio pays in) and profit (trailing yield). It shortlists the 25 best candidates by yield + 10 × months paid, then searches combinations of that shortlist for the three portfolios that best match your preference.

Get Companies by Dividend Months

Pick one or more months. A single month returns companies whose entire 2024 payout year was that month. Several months returns companies whose year was exactly that set. If nothing matches exactly, it falls back to the strongest single-month payer for each month separately.

Get Indices for a Company

Nine annual fundamentals for 246 companies across 2014–2024, gap-filled: a repeated row means "no new filing," not "no change in the business."

Recommend Growth Companies

A Random Forest predicts each company's average annual close price for 2025–2027 from its most recent fundamentals. Companies are ranked by projected return to 2027, and capital is split equally across the top of that ranking.

Model card

This is a regression on financial ratios, not a time-series model — it has no notion of momentum or market conditions. Projections are built by taking each company's latest fundamentals and only changing the year, so they answer "what would this be worth if the books stayed exactly as they are," not "what will happen." Large projected returns are usually companies whose last recorded price was low relative to what their fundamentals imply — sometimes a real mispricing, often a data-recency artefact.
